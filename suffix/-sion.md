## 📝 使用条件

**何时使用此变体**：

### 1. 词根是 **mit-/miss-** (to send)
- permit → permission
- admit → admission
- submit → submission
- transmit → transmission

### 2. 以 **-d/-de** 结尾的动词
- decide → decision
- divide → division
- extend → extension
- expand → expansion

### 3. 其他特殊情况
- collide → collision
- confuse → confusion
- impress → impression
- express → expression
